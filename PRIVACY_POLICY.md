Privacy Policy for Arya Translate
Last updated: 2026.9.14
Arya Translate respects your privacy. This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII) to the developer.
Data We Access:
The extension only accesses the text content of the web pages you actively choose to translate. This content is sent directly to our translation API provider solely for the purpose of translation and is not stored on our servers.
Local Storage:
User preferences (such as target language and UI settings) are stored locally on your device using the browser's storage API. The optional wordbook (English words you look up via selection translate) is stored in the browser by default and is never uploaded unless you configure your own cloud storage.
Optional Wordbook Sync:
If you fill in your own Alibaba Cloud OSS credentials, the wordbook JSON is uploaded only to the Bucket you specify. The developer does not receive this data. Access keys stay in your browser sync storage.
Third-Party Services:
We use qwen MT,DeepSeek mimopro for translation. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for how they handle data.
